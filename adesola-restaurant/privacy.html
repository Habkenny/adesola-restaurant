Privacy notice

Launch draft — not ready for publication. The restaurant must complete and approve this notice before collecting personal information.

Information submitted

The reservation and contact forms request information such as name, email address, preferred booking date/time, party size, and any message the visitor chooses to provide. Submissions are handled by Netlify Forms when enabled on the deployed site.

Before launch, the restaurant must add

  • The correct legal/business identity and a verified privacy contact.
  • The lawful basis and purposes for each use of submitted information.
  • Retention/deletion periods and how people can exercise their data rights.
  • Details of processors, any international transfers, and how to raise a complaint.
  • Any additional cookie/analytics disclosures and consent controls required by the final deployment.

Do not treat this draft as legal advice or collect live customer data until it has been reviewed and completed.
